Privacy Policy

AetherOli · Effective date: 4 October 2026

This policy explains how AetherOli (“the app”) handles information when you use its text and voice translation features. AetherOli is a mobile translation app developed by its publisher (“we”, “us”).

Information you enter

Text you type and translations are processed on your device by Google ML Kit’s on-device translation feature. The app does not send your translation text to an AetherOli server. Translation language models are downloaded when first needed; an internet connection is required for those downloads. After a model is available, translation runs on the device.

Recent conversation turns are kept temporarily in the app’s memory while it is open (up to 50 turns). AetherOli does not save that conversation history to an account or to an AetherOli server. It is cleared when the app process ends.

Microphone and speech recognition

If you choose voice input and grant microphone permission, Android’s speech recognition service receives audio for transcription. Depending on your device, selected recognition service, settings, and connectivity, speech recognition may be processed on the device or sent to that service. The resulting transcript is returned to AetherOli for translation. You can use the app’s text input instead and can deny or revoke microphone permission in Android settings.

Spoken playback

If you choose spoken playback, the translated text is passed to Android’s text-to-speech service selected on your device. Processing and any information handled by that service are governed by the service provider’s terms and privacy practices.

Service diagnostics

AetherOli uses Google ML Kit through Google Play services to provide translation. Google documents that ML Kit may collect device and app information, per-installation identifiers, performance information, and diagnostics or usage metrics for its services. This service information is handled by Google under its privacy practices. We do not operate an analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting backend for AetherOli.

Language model downloads and service diagnostics may require an internet connection. Google’s services may process information as described in the Google Privacy Policy and Google’s ML Kit data disclosure.

Sharing and storage

We do not sell personal information. AetherOli has no user accounts, does not run ads, and does not maintain a server that stores the text you translate. Information handled by Android speech or text-to-speech services and Google ML Kit is provided to those services to operate their features and is subject to their own policies.

Security and retention

Translation text and its result stay in the app’s temporary memory and are removed when the app process ends. The app does not keep a server-side copy. Information handled by Google ML Kit or your chosen Android speech services is retained according to those providers’ policies. The app uses the Android platform and Google Play services to download language models and deliver service communications.

Children

AetherOli is a general-purpose translation utility and is not designed specifically for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children on our own servers. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us using the address below.

Your choices

  • Use text input instead of voice input, or deny microphone permission.
  • Close the app to clear its temporary conversation history.
  • Manage Android speech recognition and text-to-speech services in your device settings.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app’s features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.
